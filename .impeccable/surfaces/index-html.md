# Home — personal proof

Primary target: `index.html` · Mode: Experience · Platform: web

## Audience and job

Visitors who meet 孟祥博 (GitHub `Jeffy-lun`) through a link on a phone or laptop: peers, friends,
collaborators. They need to recognize a person within seconds, find something worth reading, and leave
with a way to make contact. There is no verified bio, project history, employer, client, photo, or email;
authored copy is design material and must stay replaceable and claim-free.

## Chosen direction

The page is the person's working proof: a set personal statement still under revision, with an editor's
red marks and margin notes admitting the honest version. It refuses the centered-avatar portfolio and its
card grid. The memorable moment is the 校样/清样 switch: clean text becomes a marked-up draft, and the
visitor sees the gap between the polished self and the real one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal site as the author's own proof sheet — one unfinished statement, marked in red,
with the honest version in the margin — not a portfolio of cards and credentials.

OWN-WORLD: Warm proof paper (#f0ebdf), near-black ink, correction red and pencil blue only in hairline
marks; printed Song for set text, a handwritten Kai for annotations, mono for proof slugs and measures.
One continuous sheet with a binding margin, crop marks, folios, a 未定稿 stamp; layered annotation leaf
and stepped section tabs. Raised lines: one structural proof rule down the margin (curved-crease);
layered translucent leaf and stepped tab rail (acetate manual); chroma only in hairlines (iridescent
cloud); material paper ground, keyblock last (Dong Ho); one continuous sheet, never cards (suminagashi);
discrete timed steps, no fades (darkroom).

STORY: The visitor reads a polished line, notices the red corrections, understands this is a person who
edits himself in public, scrolls through abstract, now, drafts, and notes, and writes through GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On proof paper, a tall vertical 孟祥博 on the right at poster scale, a struck-through and
rewritten statement in the center, proof metadata in the left binding margin, a rotated 未定稿 stamp, and
the 校样/清样 toggle in the running head; the only primary action is to keep reading.
