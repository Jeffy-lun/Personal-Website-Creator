## Grounded list, ordered by resonance

1. **活字样本 / Type specimen** — the site as a foundry specimen sheet for the "face" 孟祥博.
   White paper, black ink, one spot colour, giant glyphs, metrics, character-set grid. A person has
   weights and quirks; the visitor "sets" them. Risk: needs a real variable font or it is type-geek costume.
2. **今日宜忌 / Personal almanac** — the site as a one-person 黄历: date, 宜/忌, solar terms.
   Red-and-black vernacular print, vertical rules, seals. A person is a set of daily dispositions;
   visiting is a ritual. Risk: horoscope gimmick, thin content, invented personality.
3. **一个人的校样 / Proof** — the site as the person's working proof: printed statement under revision,
   red editor's marks, margin notes, a 未定稿 stamp. Warm proof paper, ink, red correction, blue pencil,
   mono slugs; Song for print, Kai for hand, mono for metadata. Self as work in progress; the visitor
   reads both the official and the honest version. Signature: 校样/清样 toggle, proof line checking off
   sections as you read. Risk: annotations must earn their place or they are decoration.
4. **一个人的报纸 / Broadsheet** — masthead, dateline, columns, one-person news. The person as a
   publication. Risk: a known writer-portfolio trope; text-heavy.
5. **标本页 / Herbarium sheet** — pressed specimen, collection label, taxonomy. A life catalogued.
   Risk: static, costume; the subject becomes an object.
6. **库存清单 / Inventory** — dot-matrix ledger, kraft, barcode; person as stock. Honest itemisation.
   Risk: cold, one-note, turns a person into a SKU.
7. **测试卡 / Test card** — broadcast test pattern, schedule, channel. A person as a signal.
   Risk: retro screen gimmick; less personal.

Material families: typographic/print (1, 3, 4), vernacular calendar (2), natural history (5),
commerce/warehouse (6), broadcast/screen (7).

6. **Darkroom safelight** — audience: medium; clarity: low (needs photographs and makes the person a
   darkroom). **Declined.** Kept: a timed emergence — the stamp lands and the proof line advances in
